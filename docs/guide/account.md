# Manage your own account

Open **Account** from the menu with your name at the bottom left. Everyone can open it, whatever their role. It is about you, not the workspace, and nobody else can see or change it.

## Account tab

- **Name**: shown to the people you work with.
- **Username**: an optional second way to sign in. It needs a password.
- **Email**: type a new address and Toolshed sends a 6-digit code to it. Your email only changes after you enter the code. You can cancel a change that is waiting.
- **Delete account**: type your email to confirm. If you are the only owner of a workspace, Toolshed lists it and refuses until another owner exists or the workspace is deleted. Deleting cannot be undone.

## Security tab

- **Password**: set, change or remove it. Setting one signs you out on your other devices.
- **Two-step sign-in**: after your usual details you also enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app. You scan a picture, save your recovery codes, then enter one code to switch it on. The picture, the key and the recovery codes are shown once. Save them before leaving the page. You can turn it off, or make new recovery codes, at any time. If you have a password, Toolshed asks for it first.
- **Passkeys**: sign in with a fingerprint, face or screen lock. Add one with the button, rename it, or remove it. The button only appears in browsers that support passkeys.

Changes here need a recent sign-in. If you signed in more than 30 minutes ago the controls switch off and a button offers to sign you in again.

## Devices tab

Every browser or phone signed in to your account, newest activity first, with this one marked. Sign out any other device, or all others at once. Signing out takes effect straight away.

## While an owner is signed in as you

If an owner of the instance is looking at your account to help you, the screen says so and nothing can be changed.

## Who changed it

Each change is written to the audit log, for example `user.renamed` and `password.added`. Passwords, codes and keys are never written there.
