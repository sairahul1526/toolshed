# Users

The Users screen is for the owner of the install: the person who set Toolshed up. Nobody else can open it. Find it in the sidebar under Owner.

## Find someone

Open **Users** to see everyone who has an account, in every workspace. Type a name or email in the search box and press Enter. Long lists show 50 people at a time. Use **Next** to move on and **Back to first page** to return.

Select a name to see their details: email, workspaces, when they last signed in and whether they use two-step sign-in.

## Ban and unban

On a person's page, write a reason, choose how long, and press **Ban**. You can ban for 7 days, 30 days, or until you lift it. They are signed out everywhere straight away and cannot sign in again until the ban ends. Press **Lift the ban** to let them back in sooner.

You can't ban yourself or another owner of the install.

## Delete an account

Type the person's email in the box and press **Delete account**. This can't be undone. If they are the only owner of a workspace, Toolshed will say so and stop. Give the workspace to someone else, or delete the workspace first.

## Sign in as someone

Press **Sign in as them** to see Toolshed the way they do, for example to find out why something looks wrong for them. While you do this:

- Every action you take is logged under your name.
- You can't open the Owner screens or change security settings.
- It ends by itself after a short time. Press **Stop** in the bar at the top to end it sooner.

You can't sign in as a banned account.

## Workspaces

Open the **Workspaces** tab to see every workspace, its owner and how many people it has. Select one to rename it or change its short name. To delete a workspace and everything in it, type its short name and confirm. This can't be undone.

## If something is refused

Toolshed tells you why at the top of the page. If it asks you to sign in again, do that and repeat the action. Deleting and banning need a recent sign-in.
