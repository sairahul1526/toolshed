# Members

Members is where you see who is in the workspace, change what they can do, invite new people and remove people who no longer need access. Find it under Workspace in the sidebar.

## What you can do

- **See everyone.** The People tab lists each person with their role. Admins and owners also see whether two-step sign-in is on and when the person was last active. Search by name or email, or filter by role.
- **Change a role.** Pick a new role next to the person and press Save. The change applies the next time they open a page. Lowering a role also stops API keys and invites they made with the old role.
- **Remove someone.** They lose access to this workspace right away and are signed out of it everywhere. Their personal API keys stop working and their pending invites are cancelled. Their account stays, and so does everything they made here.
- **Leave.** Press Leave on your own row. You can come back only if an owner invites you again.
- **Invite people.** Enter an email address and a role, then press Send invite. The link works for 7 days and only for that address. The Invites tab shows each pending invite. Resend gives it a fresh 7 days, Copy link lets you share it yourself, and Cancel stops it working.

## Who can do what

- Owners and admins can invite, change roles and remove people.
- Only an owner can make someone an owner, or change or remove an owner.
- Nobody can change their own role.
- Members and viewers can see the list and leave, but not change anything.

## Good to know

- A workspace always keeps at least one owner. The last owner cannot leave or be demoted until another owner is named.
- If email sending is not set up, invites are still made but nobody gets an email. Copy the link from the Invites tab and send it yourself. Set up email under Email.
- Invites made while signed in as someone else are refused.
- The list shows up to 500 people, 50 at a time.
