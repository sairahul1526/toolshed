# Notifications

Notifications tell you what happened in your workspace: a finished export, an email problem, an update that is ready. You see them in the bell at the top of every page and on the Notifications page.

## Who sees what

Everyone who is signed in can open the page. You only see notifications that are:

- addressed to you,
- sent to every admin (if you are an admin or owner), or
- from your own workspace. You never see another workspace's notifications.

## The bell

- A dot on the bell means you have unread notifications. Screen readers hear the count, for example "Notifications, 3 new".
- Select the bell to see the latest five. Select one to read it and go to what it is about.
- "Mark all as read" clears the dot. "See all" opens the full list.
- Without JavaScript the bell is a plain link to the full list.

## The Notifications page

- The newest notifications come first. Choose **All** or **Unread** at the top.
- **Open** marks a notification as read and takes you to the page it is about. Only pages inside this app are linked; anything else is shown as plain text.
- **Mark as read** marks one. **Mark all as read** marks everything you can see.
- **Show older** loads the next 25. **Back to newest** returns to the top.

## Good to know

- Read marks are yours alone. When an admin reads a notification sent to every admin, other admins still see it as unread.
- Notifications are deleted after 60 days.
- This is the in-app list only. It does not send email or push messages.
